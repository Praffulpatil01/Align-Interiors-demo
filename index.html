The Four-Step Design Process

A transparent, disciplined path from first site conversation to the day you unlock your completed space.

01

Consultation

Discovery & Space Review

We meet at your site or our Erandwane studio to understand your daily routines, functional needs, visual preferences, and budget expectations.

02

Design

Concept & Detailed Plans

We develop tailored 2D spatial layouts, 3D visualizations, material palettes, electrical plans, and lighting schemes with full client discussion.

03

Execution

Precision Craft & Oversight

Our team manages civil work, false ceilings, electricals, carpentry, and paint finishes with rigorous on-site supervision and regular photo updates.

04

Handover

Snag Rectification & Welcome

Thorough quality snag inspection, deep professional cleaning, and a formal walkthrough ensure your home or office is delivered ready to live in.

About the Studio

An architecture and interior studio grounded in listening and considered craft.

Based in Erandwane, Pune, Align Architecture & Interiors approaches residential and commercial spaces as harmonious environments built around practical human movement, natural light, and quiet materials.

We do not believe in forcing a generic visual style onto a home or workplace. A successful interior is not simply photogenic on handover day; it is comfortable, functional, and durable years into daily living.

From the dimensions of kitchen counters and wardrobe depths to the acoustic softness of living rooms and the placement of switchboards, every choice is deliberate.

“Thoughtfully designed. Beautifully lived in.”

The Align Studio Motto
Studio Principal & Lead Designer

Rakesh Dashpute

“Good interior architecture does not seek to shout; it resolves spatial puzzles and stands the test of daily living.”

As the lead designer at Align Architecture & Interiors, Rakesh brings a hands-on, practical methodology to residential and commercial projects across Pune. Believing that homes should nurture rather than complicate daily routines, his work balances structural clarity, clever storage solutions, durable natural textures, and meticulous on-site craftsmanship.

From initial spatial concepts to structural modifications, bespoke joinery detailing, and final snagging, Rakesh personally oversees project milestones to ensure that design ideas translate reliably into lived reality.
