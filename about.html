About the Studio

An architecture and interior studio grounded in listening and considered craft.

Based in Erandwane, Pune, Align Architecture & Interiors approaches residential and commercial spaces as harmonious environments built around practical human movement, natural light, and quiet materials.

Our Core Philosophy

We do not believe in forcing a generic visual style onto a home or workplace. A successful interior is not simply photogenic on handover day; it is comfortable, functional, and durable years into daily living.

From the dimensions of kitchen counters and wardrobe depths to the acoustic softness of living rooms and the placement of switchboards, every choice is deliberate. We design spaces that feel natural, calm, and intrinsically aligned with the families and professionals who inhabit them.

“Thoughtfully designed. Beautifully lived in.”

The Align Motto

How We Collaborate

Transparent Working Principles

How we manage scope, budgets, communication, and site execution with our clients.

Listening to Real Needs

We dedicate time to understand who will live or work in each room, everyday storage pain points, work-from-home routines, and lighting expectations before drawing lines.

Budget Alignment & Material Choice

Project scope, hardware grades, veneer selections, laminate finishes, and lighting budgets are comprehensively evaluated and agreed upon with you prior to commencing site procurement.

Active Site Communication

As verified by client reviews, we provide regular progress updates via WhatsApp and site visits, ensuring you are never left guessing about current milestones.

Disciplined Handover

A systematic snag checklist, alignment verification of all custom hardware and doors, and thorough cleaning take place before we formally hand over the keys.
